### DuckDB's quantile_cont is stated as what it computes, and its route is a shim

`quantile_interp`'s `LINEAR` (DuckDB's own `quantile_cont`) now takes the neighbours of its double position `(n − 1)·q` and an ulp past them where the lower neighbour is first, instead of "between the neighbours". The written plan's guards (`Dialect.quantile`) are the shim `quantile_guard`, with the precondition `(COUNT(x) − 1)·10^scale < 2^52` checked on the data, so a DuckDB `quantile_cont` route reports class `shim` where it reported `exact` (the evidence stays tested, tracked in [#900](https://github.com/AlexanderNenninger/substrait-lean/issues/900)).
