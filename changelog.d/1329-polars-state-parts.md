### Polars keeps a pipeline view's state in Parquet parts, as Spark does

On Polars, a refresh now writes the change as a new Parquet part and leaves the state's other parts where they are; the Lean merge plan adds the parts up when something reads the table or they pass a size bound (#1329, #173). Polars and Spark share one implementation of the parts (`sverify._parts`). Four inserts of a quarter of the rows each into a filter view peak at 695 MB where they peaked at 947 MB over two million rows, and at 1369 MB where they peaked at 2314 MB over eight million; a refresh of a built state costs the change, 0.1 s for 100,000 rows at either size.
