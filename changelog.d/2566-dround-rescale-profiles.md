### Polars's profile states its own decimal rescale

A cast of a decimal to a smaller scale rounds half to even on Polars ([polars-decimal-casts](engines/polars/facts/polars-decimal-casts.json)), and half away from zero in the reference, yet Polars's profile stated the reference's cast, and the generated lowering wrote Polars's native cast as proved. `Profile.polars` now rounds that cast half to even, as its table states, and the lowering writes the reference's value from Polars's truncation, as the `decimal_round_half_away` shim does. A check over each engine's tables keeps every native entry's options at the reference's behaviour ([#2566](https://github.com/AlexanderNenninger/substrait-lean/issues/2566)). The values sverify writes and runs are unchanged: Polars's run path already shimmed the cast.
