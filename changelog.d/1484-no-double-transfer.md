### Plans without doubles are proved near-safe on every engine

Sverify proves that a plan in which no double occurs gives, on an engine's table, only results the reference allows, where the table with the reference's choices at the options only doubles read transfers (`Rel.transfer_of_noDouble`, [#1484](https://github.com/AlexanderNenninger/substrait-lean/issues/1484)). The run plan's `near` gate now rests on that theorem: under `floats_plan` or an `approx` postcondition, a rewrite runs only where neither plan has a double or an open measure and both transfer at DuckDB, Polars and Spark. Plans the theorem does not cover keep their `run_gate` of `near`: an integer `AVG` at Polars or Spark, and a plan with an integer `BIT_AND`, `BIT_OR`, `BIT_XOR` or `QUANTILE_DISC`, which is not closed.
